## 2 · What to click (demo order)

1. **Visa stepper (0 → 1 → 2)** — the 0-and-1-visa story; that's ~95% of companies.
2. **Licence term (1 / 2 / 3 / 5)** — watch the committed total change. SPC has a real **1%-incremental** licence discount; SHAMS shows **no** multi-year discount (terms not published).
3. **Base zone selector** — switch off Meydan (e.g. to IFZA); every column reorders and all "vs" rows + the read-out re-point.
4. **Show Zones filter** — focus the comparison on a subset of zones.
5. **Hover-comment** — hover any cell, click the marker, add a note (e.g. IFZA's 12,900 → "possible offer 11,900"). Persists via Download; clears on Reset.
6. **Edit data** — change a figure live; it flows through the whole table + read-out. Download to save, Reset to revert.
7. **Amber underline** = an assumed / modeling-choice value (IFZA medical & EID; SPC/SHAMS investor visa + change of status). Confirmed figures render plain.
