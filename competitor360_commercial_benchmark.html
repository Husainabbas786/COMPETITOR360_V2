The headline is a trap

  The renewal row is the quiet kicker: Meydan re-charges licence + allocation + visa every year, while IFZA's visa is free-for-life and Ajman renews cheaper — so Meydan's gap  widens  over a 3-year horizon.

The other half of the war

RAKEZ pays a partner 50% from the very first deal. Meydan starts at 30% and only reaches 50% above 121 sales a quarter — so for the typical partner, Meydan is the least rewarding zone to sell.
